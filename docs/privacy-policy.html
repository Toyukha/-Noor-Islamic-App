1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Noor (com.aistudio.noorislamic.zpqtwv) is built as a reverent, ad-free Islamic companion for daily Salah tracking, prayer time calculation, offline Quran reading, Adhkar remembrance, and Ramadan worship goals.

We do not display third-party advertisements, we do not include advertising trackers, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

Core features—including the complete 604-page offline Uthmani Mushaf, Makkah/Fajr Adhan audio, Surah Al-Kahf, Surah Al-Mulk, Surah Al-Waqi'ah, and local prayer schedules—work without tracking your reading habits across external websites or apps.

2. Information We Process & Collect

  • Optional Google Sign-In Account Information: If you choose to sign in with Google via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication, Noor receives your basic authentication identifier (Firebase UID), display name, and email address strictly to authenticate your session and sync your worship progress.
  • User-Authored Worship Logs & Preferences: When signed in, your selected city, prayer calculation method, prayer completion records, Quran Khatm progress, and earned achievement badges may be synced to Google Cloud Firestore so your progress can be restored across sessions.
  • Optional Device Location (GPS): If you tap Auto-Detect My City via GPS, Noor requests your coarse or fine location once to resolve your nearest city and coordinates for accurate Salah and Qibla/astronomical prayer timings. Location is never used for advertising or background behavioral profiling.

4. Offline & On-Device Storage

Noor stores the majority of your private worship data directly on your device using Android Room (SQLite) and local app preferences:

  • Daily obligatory and voluntary prayer logs, fasting records, and streak counts.
  • Your Tadabbur personal reflections, Quran reader bookmarks, Day/Night Mushaf display toggle, and Khatm pacing settings.
  • Local PDF/CSV progress reports you choose to export and share using the Android system share sheet.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

Noor relies strictly on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to provide optional Google Sign-In and secure cloud backup/sync of your account settings and worship records. Data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

Noor does not integrate advertising networks, behavioral analytics brokers, or third-party social tracking SDKs.

6. Voluntary Sadaqah & External Payment Links

Noor is free and maintained through voluntary community support (Sadaqah Jariyah). When you tap the donation button in Settings, Noor opens the external Flutterwave payment page (flutterwave.com/donate/udnd7pfu6sbt) in your device's web browser.

Noor never collects, processes, views, or stores debit/credit card numbers, bank account credentials, or payment receipts. Any voluntary payment is handled solely by Flutterwave under Flutterwave's own privacy and security policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Noor is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) without parental consent. If you believe a child has created a cloud account without authorization, please contact us so we can promptly delete the record.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You stay in full control of your information and can delete your data at any time:

  • On-Device Data: Open your device's Android Settings → Apps → Noor → Storage → Clear Data, or uninstall the app, to permanently remove all local Room records and preferences.
  • Cloud Account & Firestore Data Deletion: To permanently delete your Firebase Authentication record and synced Firestore documents without using the app, email oyeyemiyunus24@gmail.com from (or specifying) the Google account email associated with Noor. Verified deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.
